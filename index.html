What the portal contains

Content is posted by HPAIR alumni and by the current board. The portal is visible only to signed-in members and is not indexed by search engines.

Directory
Registered alumni, searchable by name, class year, industry, conference and city. A profile can include a photograph, a short biography, current position, a LinkedIn link, and whether the member is available for mentoring.
Opportunities
Job, internship, fellowship and scholarship notices posted by members, each with a closing date. HPAIR posts its own calls for partners, speakers and host institutions here.
Updates
Announcements from the current board, including conference dates, calls for volunteers and news about alumni.
Conferences
Dates for the next Harvard Conference, Asia Conference and Virtual Conference, a photo archive that members can add to, and the post-conference feedback form.
Ambassadors
The Alumni Ambassador application. Ambassadors serve for one year and represent HPAIR in their city or region.

Asia Conference host cities

HPAIR has held the Harvard Conference in Cambridge since 1991 and the Asia Conference in a partner city across the Asia-Pacific since 1992. Members list the conferences they attended on their profiles, and the directory can be filtered by conference.

Select a year to turn the globe to that city.

    Questions

    Who can register

    Anyone who has taken part in an HPAIR conference as a delegate, staff member, speaker or partner. Create an account with your email address and a password.

    Which email address to use

    Use an address you check regularly. Password reset emails are sent to it.

    Who can see your profile

    Profiles are visible only to signed-in members and are not indexed by search engines. You can choose whether other members see your email address, and every field other than your name is optional.

    Resetting your password

    Select “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in page. A reset link is sent to your email address. If it does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

    Posting a job or fellowship

    After signing in, go to Opportunities and select “Post”. You can include a closing date, an application link and a PDF. Postings can be edited or removed at any time.

    Technical problems

    Email tech-help@hpair.org describing what you were trying to do and which browser you were using. A screenshot is helpful.
